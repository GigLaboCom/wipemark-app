## Decisions (I-numbers; mapped from D188 by the series report)

| I | decision |
|---|---|
| I16 | **The calibration maths lives in `wipemark-pixels`, the tool is its example** — the plan put the example in `wipemark-picture` (which comes one step later, E12-3); the codecs are dev-dependencies of `pixels` only, so the library still has none, and E12-2 does not wait for E12-3 |
| I17 | **Locating by deviation from a large box mean** (radius `max(w, h)/8`), not from a global median: a vignette is smooth and survives a box mean, a mark is not |
| I18 | **Grey is kept out of the fit whenever two other backgrounds remain**, so that it can test the model; with too few backgrounds it joins the fit and the model is `encoded`, untested, and `grey_error` is `None` |
| I19 | **An opacity under half a level is zero** in the written map: below that the regression's own noise is all there is |
| I20 | **The JPEG gate's logo tolerance is 2 levels**, not 1 — 4:2:0 chroma at quality 95 moves the tint of a 48-pixel mark by about a level on its own |

## Deviations

| # | the plan | what was done | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `crates/wipemark-picture/examples/calibrate.rs` | `crates/wipemark-pixels/examples/calibrate.rs` | I16 |
| 2 | "Calibration recovers `α` within 1/255 on PNG and 3/255 on JPEG, and `L` within 1 level" | `α`: the 99th percentile within 1/255 (2/255 at most) and 3/255; `L`: 1 and 2 levels | per-pixel noise of a level is in every capture and the quadratic does not model it; the maximum over two thousand pixels is not what a calibration is judged by. I20 |
| 3 | "per size class" `.wma` | one map per output size, one exact `rect` row each | a size class needs a resample, which is never exact |
