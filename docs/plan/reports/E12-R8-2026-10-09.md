How it works, where the document left a choice:

* **The working space is the file's.** A JPEG whose planes are read is refined
  in them: Y at full resolution with `α`, Cb and Cr at their own with the
  block's `ᾱ`. On a subsampled JPEG the start is R6's `planar::Inverse` (its
  `y_out`, `cb_out`, `cr_out`); on a 4:4:4 JPEG under DCT-POCS an inverse in
  the same shape is built over R0's restoration (a block is a pixel, the
  restored values before R0's rounding). Either is written back through R6's
  `Inverse::rgb_at` — the decoder's upsampler and colour constants, rounded
  and clamped once. A lossy WebP, a JPEG whose planes did not read, and pixel
  POCS / Wiener on a 4:4:4 JPEG work in RGB. `Interval.space` says which.
* **The region** is the mark's rectangle widened to the codec's grid (8·`sx`
  by 8·`sy`: 8 at 4:4:4, 16 at 4:2:0) and 8 samples around it for the filter.
  A sample moves only if the restoration writes it (R0's mask, or R6's); the
  rest are the file's.
* **`P_D`** (DCT-POCS), per whole 8 × 8 block holding a sample the
  restoration writes: composite the estimate forward, clamp each coefficient
  into `[(q − ½)Q, (q + ½)Q]`, transform back, put every sample the
  restoration does **not** write back to the file's value, and repeat until
  both hold (largest violation ≤ 10⁻⁶; at most 5 000 inner rounds — 200 left
  4 of the 8 crops at 0.7–2.3·10⁻⁴); then unblend. The samples outside the
  mark have to stay the file's (`prove` refuses a sample moved outside the
  rectangle), so the data set is "the intervals **and** the anchor": the
  decoded block is in both by construction, so the intersection is never
  empty. A block not whole at the grid's edge is not refined.
* **`consistency_dct`** counts, over every whole block the restoration writes
  in, the coefficients further than 10⁻⁴ outside their interval, measured on
  the value chosen (before the one rounding to RGB levels, which
  `consistency_px` then sees). It is 0 after any DCT-POCS round.
* **Pixel POCS** clamps into `I ± h` per sample of the working space,
  carried through the inverse, `h = 0.5 + 2·σ` with σ that channel's
  `sigma_base`. **Wiener** is one step,
  `O = O₀·s/(s + n) + P_S(O₀)·n/(s + n)`, `n = σ²/(1 − α)²`, `s` the variance
  of the ring's Laplacian over √20.
* **The text rule**: the first channel's Laplacian energy over the samples the
  restoration writes, each weighted by `1 − α` (which takes the inverse's
  amplification of the codec's error back off), against the ring's.
* **When it stops**: before each round, when `texture / texture_around` is in
  `[0.8, 1.2]`; after one, when the estimate moved less than 0.1 at p95; and —
  **a deviation** — a round that leaves the restoration under 0.8 of its
  surroundings is **taken back** (the round before it ended on `P_D` too).
  When no round is kept (in the band from the start, or the first round
  taken back), today's restoration stands **byte for byte** with every
  measure it had, and `iterations` is 0; `consistency_dct` is then that
  restoration's own share outside the intervals.
* **After a refinement** every measure is taken again on the raster written:
  outline, steps, chroma, texture, `texture_left`, `smoothed`, `changed`,
  `clamped`, and `consistency_px` through R7's `verify::consistency` — in the
  planes via R6's `Inverse::blend_back` on the planar path, in RGB (the very
  pairs R0 makes) on the RGB path. `exact` stays false (lossy only).
* **The second pass** (D165) is not refined: its raster is no longer the file's.

## Deviations from the document

   `wipemark-pixels/tests/interval.rs`: a pixels test cannot decode a file
   (§3 row 4, D501). The synthetic ones are in pixels'.
2. **`restore` keeps its signature**; `restore_refined` and `clean_refined`
   take `RestoreOptions` (and the planes DCT-POCS needs).
3. **`Interval` carries two more fields**, `space` (`"ycbcr"`/`"rgb"`: where
   σ was measured — the numbers mean different things) and `text` (which
   radius was used).
4. **A round that would end under 0.8 of the surroundings is taken back**,
   and **no round kept leaves today's restoration byte for byte**.

   values** (an inner alternation to 10⁻⁶): `prove` refuses any sample moved
   outside the mark's rectangle, and the document's widened ROI reaches there.

   start and model); in RGB elsewhere.
7. **The text rule weights the restored samples' Laplacian by `1 − α`**; the
   document does not say what the ROI's energy is measured on.
