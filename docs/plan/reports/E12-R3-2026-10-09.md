### The decoder that makes the raster is the decoder that makes the planes

`wipemark-picture` decodes every JPEG with its own `zune-jpeg = "0.5"`
dependency (`decode.rs`, `jpeg_decode`), and `prove` decodes what `clean`
wrote through the same `decode`. `image` 0.25.10's `zune-jpeg` 0.4.21 is
a separate package the `[patch]` does not reach; `wipemark-picture` uses
`image` only for its JPEG *encoder*, and the tests that read a JPEG back
through `image` never ask for planes. So the planes come from the decoder
that produces the raster (checked: `Cargo.lock` with the patch shows 0.5.15
from the path, 0.4.21 from crates.io; `the_rgb_raster_did_not_move` holds
the raster to the unpatched decoder's).

### The patch (D301)

* **Opened:** `JpegDecoder::decode_planes(&mut self) ->
  Result<JpegPlanes, DecodeErrors>`, `pub struct JpegPlanes { width,
  height, components: Vec<PlaneOut>, qt: [Option<[u16; 64]>; 4],
  restart_interval: u16, progressive: bool }`, `pub struct PlaneOut { id,
  h, v, qt_index, width, height, samples: Vec<u8> }` — the shape §4.1
  gives, `#[derive(Clone, Debug, PartialEq, Eq)]`. Nothing of upstream's
  changed visibility.
* **How:** a crate-private `want_planes` flag, set only inside
  `decode_planes`; `post_process` — where every path (baseline, baseline
  with a scan per component, progressive) ends a row of MCUs — copies each
  component's `raw_coeff` rows into its plane, cropped to `ceil(W·h/h_max)
  × ceil(H·v/v_max)`, and returns before `upsample` and `color_convert`.
  The flag also makes every component `needed` (three conditions) and keeps
  the YCbCr→Luma `coeff = 2` shortcut out (two conditions).
* **Size:** `src/planes.rs` new, 196 lines (about 80 documentation); 21
  lines added in `decoder.rs`, `lib.rs`, `mcu.rs`, `mcu_prog.rs`; none
  removed. Local, not deep: no decision against a C library is needed
  (§9).
* **Its own tests:** the crate's 22 (unit and doc tests) pass;
  `cargo clippy` gives the same 105 warnings with and without the patch,
  none in `planes.rs`.
* **The base** is the crate as published on crates.io (the container
  cannot clone GitHub), whose `.cargo_vcs_info.json` names upstream
  `31d81fed7551c8ccea456d9d8e2b1fd8bebb6995`. The patch was checked to
  apply with `patch -p1 --dry-run` over a fresh copy of the published
  sources laid out as the monorepo (`crates/zune-jpeg/…`, `PATCH.md` at
  the root).

### `Planes` (D302)

As §4.2, with these choices: `Plane::new` refuses a sample over 255 and a
length that is not `width × height`; `Planes::new` refuses a plane of
another size than its sampling makes, chroma missing (or present on
`Gray`), and factors that are 0, over 4 or do not divide the largest;
`Sampling::of(h, v)` names the forms by their ratios (4:4:4 written as
2 × 2 three times is `H444`); `Quant::chroma` is the table Cb and Cr
**share** — a JPEG whose Cb and Cr name two tables gets no planes rather
than half of them. `Plane::get(x, y)` and `Sampling::id()` were added.
`to_rgb` restates `upsampler/scalar.rs`, `worker.rs` (the edge rows) and
`color_convert/scalar.rs` with a D45-style header.

## Findings

1. **An even side reads the MCU padding.** `zune-jpeg` upsamples a row of
   the *padded* plane; the last output column of an even width (not a
   whole number of MCUs) is an odd output leaning on chroma sample
   `ceil(W/2)`, which is past the cropped plane, in the padding. Likewise
   the last row of an even height at 4:2:0. `to_rgb` repeats the last
   sample there and differs by up to 9 levels at 38 × 24. An odd side never
   asks for it (its last output is even and leans inward), and a whole
   number of MCUs ends the decoder's own row where it repeats its last
   sample as `to_rgb` does — so the 1040 crops (65 MCUs) are exact too.
   The spec's fixtures are odd, which hid it; the even fixture is kept on
   purpose. For R6 it does not matter where the mark is (never the last
   column or row); it is written down in `zune-jpeg-pin.md` §4 and Q-R3a
   below.
2. **No rounding-order slack was needed.** `to_rgb` is the decoder to the
   byte on every odd fixture; §5's "within 1 level" clause was not used.
3. **The planes cost a second decode of ×1.4, not ×1.1.** `decode_planes`
   is the entropy decoding, dequantisation and IDCT again, and the planes
   are then widened to `u16`: 233 ms over the 21 against 611 ms for the
   whole RGB decode (scan walk, upsampling and conversion included), so
   about 0.38 of a decode. A second pass of anything that size cannot
   stay within ×1.10; §6.3's own rule then applies (Deviation 1).
4. **Architecture.** This container is aarch64: the pinned hashes and the
   byte equality were measured through `zune-jpeg`'s NEON paths, and the
   scalar restatement matching them shows NEON and scalar agree. The AVX2
   paths (x86-64 CI) compute the same integers by their source (the same
   14-bit constants, `madd` then `>> 14`), but nothing here has run them:
   the first x86-64 run of `planes.rs` is the check.

## Deviations from the document

1. **`decode` takes no planes; `decode_with_planes` does** (§4.3 against
   §6.3). §4.3 has `jpeg_decode` run `decode_planes` and set `planes`;
   §6.3 says that if the cost is over ×1.10, "the planes are taken only
   when a mark is verified, by a second decode in R6". Measured ×1.397, so
   the product road (`decode`, and through it `clean`, `inspect` and the
   proof) leaves `planes` `None` and costs what it cost; `decode_with_planes`
   is the second decode, ready for R6 to call once a mark on a JPEG is
   verified. Every test of §5 runs through it, and the routing and raster
   tests run through both. Q-R3b asks whether a single-pass variant is
   wanted instead.
2. **The tables are read by a marker walk in the test, not through
   `wipemark_image` blocks** (§5, third row): `wipemark_image`'s blocks are
   `pub(crate)` and its public API exposes metadata findings only. The
   walk shares no code with the decoder, which is what "independently"
   asks.
3. **"Two DQT segments" is the default, so a one-segment fixture was
   added.** libjpeg writes every table in a DQT segment of its own; every
   Pillow fixture therefore has two. `make.py` rewrites one file so both
   tables sit in **one** segment, and adds a 16-bit table under SOF1.
4. **Extra fixtures and tests:** an even size (Finding 1), a second grey
   and a second q100 picture, 129 × 65 at all three samplings, the
   Gemini crops' JPEGs in the byte-equality test; two tests beyond the
   table (above).
5. **`Quant` holds one chroma table**, so Cb and Cr on two tables gives no
   planes (§4.2 says "the two quantisation tables" and does not say what
   happens then).
6. **`Raster::trusted_rgb8`** (`pub(crate)`, `raster.rs`, not in the list
   of files touched): `to_rgb` builds a raster it has already made in range
   and to size, without a `Result` it could never return.
7. **The fork's first commit is the published crate, not upstream's
   history** (no clone of GitHub here); `PATCH.md` says so and how the
   patch goes onto the real tag.
8. **`fixtures/image/README.md` and `.gitattributes`** gained a section and
   a line for the new directory.

The document's §3 facts all held against the code and `zune-jpeg`
0.5.15's sources (line numbers included).

## Questions

* **Q-R3a — the even edge.** Should `PlaneOut` keep one sample of padding
  past each cropped side (or `Planes` carry the decoder's edge neighbour),
  so that `to_rgb` is the decoder on every size? Today it is the decoder on
  every odd size and every whole number of MCUs, and off by up to 9 levels
  in the last column or row otherwise. Proposed: no — R6 writes back only
  inside a mark's rectangle and leaves the decoder's raster elsewhere —
  but R6's report should say so.
* **Q-R3b — one decode instead of two.** A variant of the patch could copy
  the planes *and* go on upsampling and converting in the same decode
  (`want_planes` without the early return), for about the cost of a copy
  (an estimate; not built, not measured).
  It changes the call the raster comes from (§4.3 asks for "the same
  call") and makes the patch a little larger. Worth it only if R6 wants the
  planes for every JPEG rather than for a verified mark.
* **Q-R3c — Cb and Cr on two tables.** Such a JPEG gets no planes. Should
  `Quant` carry Cb's and Cr's tables apart instead?

## Decisions proposed

* **D301** — as `E12-R-recon.md` §5.2, with: the fork's branch is cut from
  the published 0.5.15 (`31d81fe`), the patch is the one in
  `E12-R3-zune-jpeg-planes.patch` and the fork's `PATCH.md`, and

  the pin is a local path patch that is never committed.
* **D302** — as `E12-R-recon.md` §5.2, amended by the measurement:
  `Decoded.planes` is filled by **`decode_with_planes`** only; `decode`
  leaves it `None`, because the second decode costs ×1.40 of the first on
  a 2048 JPEG at q95 4:2:0, over the ×1.10 §6.3 allows. `Planes::to_rgb` is
  the decoder to the byte on every odd side and every whole number of
  MCUs; on any other even side its last column or row differs where the
  decoder read the MCU padding (up to 9 levels at 38 × 24), which is
  written down rather than a defect. `Quant` holds the chroma table Cb and
  Cr share; a JPEG whose Cb and Cr differ gets none.
