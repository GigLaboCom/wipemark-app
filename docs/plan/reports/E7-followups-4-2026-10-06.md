### Z2 — D290's stale-look guard, and the seam

**The seam.** The landing of a look — the closure body inside
`PanelView::look` — is now a method, `PanelView::landed(arrivals, index,
plan, found, cx) -> bool`, which `look` calls exactly where the closure did.
Nothing else moved: the same `held_for`, the same `held.plans[index] ==
plan` test, the same `notify`, the same "still held" answer that stops the
rest of a drop's task.

**Why a seam, and why this one.** The task suggested a gate on the read
(as `Cleaner::run`) or a first read that blocks on a channel. Neither works
under `#[gpui::test]`: the test dispatcher runs background tasks on the test
thread, so a read that blocks blocks the test itself; and which of two
ready looks lands first is the dispatcher's seeded choice, not the test's —
a test relying on it would be green with the guard deleted on half the
seeds. What the guard protects is a *landing*, so the test lands one: a
look taken for real by the old plan (`clean::inspect_one` by the held
*Beside* plan — the U+200B found), handed to `landed` **after** the page was
switched to *In place* and the newer look (the link refusal) had landed
through the real road.

**The test.** `link.md` dropped under *Beside*; its plan and a real look by
it kept aside; the page switched to *In place* and settled (refusal held,
no would-lines); the old look landed through `landed` → it answers "still
held", and the panel still holds the refusal, paints no would-lines, and its
held plan is the page's plan and not *Beside*.
