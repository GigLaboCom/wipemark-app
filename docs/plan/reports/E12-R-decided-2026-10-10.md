## D470 — the search does not prove what its row refused by gain

**The rule** (`crates/wipemark-pixels/src/lib.rs`, `gain_refused_here`;
`ROW_PLACE` in `propose.rs`). In `examine_pass`, when no row's mark was
proved and the search proves one, the proof is not taken if a finding of
the **same profile** placed by a **row** was refused by
**`Refusal::Gain`** and the search's rectangle has **the same centre** as
that row's to within `ROW_PLACE` = 3/16 px on both axes, whatever the
size. The row's refusal is then the finding; the search's proof is
dropped.

**Why this rule and not a looser one.** The first version — "a gain
refusal anywhere the search's rectangle overlaps (IoU > 0.3)" — closed the
`k = 0.93` test and turned
`a_mark_half_a_pixel_off_its_row_is_proved_by_the_search` red: a real
`k = 1` mark half a pixel (or a quarter, both ways) off its row is also
refused by its row **by gain** (the half-cancelled edges vanish at another
`k`), and the search proves it where it is. What tells the two apart is
the place. Measured with the rule off
(`a_weaker_mark_through_the_rows_and_the_search`, every case proved by the
search, 8 kinds × 3 seeds × k 0.90/0.93/0.95): V1-48 moved its origin by at
most ⅛ px at the same size; V2-36 shrank by up to 0.875 px **about the same
centre** (origin moved up to 0.5 px, centre ≤ 0.0625 px). Every one is
within ⅛ px of its row's centre. A real mark off its row moves the centre
by ¼ px or more. The sweeps put origins and sizes on eighths, so centres
fall on sixteenths; the bound sits between ⅛ and ¼, at 3/16.

What it does not cover, by design: a mark **both** weaker and off its row
(not at the row's place — the owner's option a speaks of "a row at the
same place"); and a row refused for another reason (edges, out of range),
after which the search still proves as before. A resampled mark (the
corpus's `recon-resized`, the frames) is at another place and size: its
row sees no blend there (no finding) or another profile refuses it, so
nothing changes — confirmed on all 63 resized files and 189 frames.

**Tests.**
`a_k_of_0_93_is_refused_by_a_k_of_1_profile` (`crates/wipemark-pixels/tests/verify.rs`)
is a normal test now. One change to it: its tolerance on the row's `k*`
became `0.03 + 1e-4`, as its sibling `the_benchs_gain_is_the_gain_a_row_measures`
has — V2-36 over the fractal reads `k* = 0.90`, 0.03 from 0.93 in decimals

`a_mark_half_a_pixel_off_its_row_is_proved_by_the_search` (`tests/exact.rs`)

`a_weaker_mark_through_the_rows_and_the_search` (`tests/measure.rs`,

## D472 — DCT-POCS by default

`wipemark_picture::REFINE: Refine = Refine::Dct`; `clean` is
`clean_bytes_refined(…, RestoreOptions { refine: REFINE })`. No
environment variable is read (`preview_refine` is gone). `Refine`'s
`Default` stays `None` in the library, so the bench's `R0`/`R6` and
`clean_bytes_with_planes` keep the inverse's value; the product asks for
DCT-POCS by name. Pixel POCS and Wiener stay in `interval.rs` for `R8p`
and `R8w`. On a lossless source nothing runs (S6, `a_lossless_source_is_never_refined`).

**Lossy WebP.** DCT-POCS needs a JPEG's planes and tables; a WebP has
none, so `interval::setup` returns nothing and the restoration is the
inverse's — what R8d did on the bench's `webp-lossy-q90` (R6's numbers).
On the corpus `recon-webp:fixture-q90` carries no `interval` and keeps its
outline and texture said, as before. Kept as it is.

`Restored.interval` and `consistency_dct` are R8's JSON, unchanged; they
now appear on every restoration of a JPEG whose planes read. D307's lower
bound stays live: no restoration of the corpus and no committed crop is
`smoothed`, through the product or the inverse alone
(`no_restoration_is_smoothed`, renamed from `…_on_todays_path_…`).
