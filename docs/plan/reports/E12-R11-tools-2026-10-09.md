## 1. What was built

| file | what |

### Deviations, and one finding about R11's table

* **The reading does not use `std/std_ring` alone.** For one fixed blend
  `std_i I_i(p) = (1 − α(p))·std_i O_i(p)`, so `std/std_ring ≈ 1 − α`. It is
  "≪ 1" only where the mark is nearly opaque: a half-transparent wordmark at
  α 0.5 reads 0.5 whether its opacity is fixed or drawn per file, so R11
  §4.1's first and third rows cannot be told apart by that ratio. The script
  writes `ratio.png` and the ratio's median as R11 asks, and reads the table
  on `q` (the scatter one blend leaves unexplained, over the floor where
  there is no mark) and on which per-file model explains it. The self-test
  holds the ratio to `1 − α̂` on a fixed mark rather than to "≪ 1". **For the
  owner**: R11 §4.1's row 1 might say "the scatter a fixed blend predicts"
  rather than "`std/std_ring ≪ 1`"; nothing else depends on it.
* **The ring has a 2 px guard** (`--guard`), because at stage 1 the
  rectangle is a person's estimate and the position may wander;
  `--guard 0` is calibrate's ring exactly.
* **Crop margins are per axis** (1.5 × the mark's width left and right,
  1.5 × its height above and below), not 1.5 × one size: for a wide
  wordmark one size would be either a sliver above it or a crop many times
  its area. Clipped at the picture's edge, the same for every file of a
  pair.
* **Three files, not two**: `README.md` beside the two scripts, as the step
  names; `align.py` imports `invariance.py` (one reader, one crop, one
  quadratic) rather than a third module.
* **The negatives test examines the words under the shipped profiles too.**
  `no_procedural_negative_is_ever_restored` runs against
  `synthetic_catalogue()` (the test stand-ins `test-sparkle-v1/v2`), not the
  shipped Gemini profiles, as the brief assumed. The word family is examined
  under both; the other families keep the synthetic catalogue alone.
* **The word pictures are 160 × 160**, not the others' 96: a word 30–110 px
  wide (R2 §4.2) with a margin does not fit 96.

## 3. The word negatives

`negative(n)` gets a seventh slot: `n % 7 == 6` is a word of two to five
letters, 30–110 px wide, 4–24 px from the bottom and right edges of a
160 × 160 picture of the slot's kind, in three styles taken in turn
(`(n / 7) % 3`): **opaque** white (`stamp_opaque`, hard-edged — no blend),
**outlined** (the same over a dark, 0–60, one-pixel outline), and
**half-transparent** white at a peak of 0.3–0.7 (`composite` — a white blend,
but not a mark's). The count goes from 2000 to **2331** (333 × 7), so the four
families before it keep their counts (1001/333/333/333 → 999/333/333/333) and
the words are 333 (111 per style). Because the families moved from `n % 6` to
`n % 7`, which `n` is which negative changed for every family; the families
themselves are unchanged.

The test's assertions on a word: never restored and the raster unmoved
(`never_restored`, as every negative); an opaque or outlined word never
reported (D235); a half-transparent word may be **seen** (refused) and never
**proved** (every finding `Refused`). Not vacuous: each word negative must
differ from its bare picture in at least 20 pixels, and the family must hold
`NEGATIVES / 7` words.

|---|---|---|
| negatives | 2000 × 2 profiles × 2 sources: **3272** proposals, every one no blend; 0 reported, 0 restored | 2331 × 2 × 2: *pending* proposals; 0 reported but half-transparent words, 0 restored (expected) |
| words | — | 333 (111 opaque, 111 outlined, 111 half-transparent) × (2 synthetic + 2 shipped profiles) × 2 sources: *pending* proposals; half-transparent *pending* of 444 examinations seen; **0 verified, 0 restored** (expected) |
| planar, 300 + 300 | `{("edges","edges"): 43, ("gain","gain"): 171}` | *pending* — the first 300 negatives now hold 43 words, and its doc comment's 1000-each figures are marked as measured before the word family |
| wallpapers | 120 × 2 × 2: 180 proposals, 0 reported | unchanged (not touched) |
| look-alike blends | 1442 of 2000 reported, lowest edge ratio 0.143; 994 of 1000 by their gain | unchanged (not touched) |

The test took 166 s at the base (`opt-level = 3` for `wipemark-pixels` in
every profile); the words add 333 pictures of 160² under four profiles, so
expect it a little over 200 s. **If any word is verified or restored, that
is a false positive of the profiles on text — a finding to report, not a
threshold to tune.**
