## Deviations from the prompt, and why

1. **`ValidationContext` holds `ctx_len`, not `max_tokens`**, plus
   `other_role` and `based_on`. `ctx_len: Option<u32>` is the model's
   window, named as `EngineInfo::ctx_len` names it; the limit is a tenth
   of it. `other_role` is needed because `{PROTECTED}` is required once
   per *step* — neither template can be judged alone. `based_on` drives W4.
2. **`has_protected` is derived from the text**, not passed in
   (`has_placeholder`): an argument can disagree with the text it
   describes, and for step 2 the text is step 1's answer.
3. **Intensity clauses and the other fragments are not editable** in v1.
   D74's key names `(lang, tactic, step, role)` only; the notes' "intensity
   clauses are templates too" is deferred. A user who wants other wording
   drops `{INTENSITY}` and writes their own.
4. **R6 refuses any `⟦` or `⟧`**, not only a `⟦digits⟧` literal: the
   brackets are the document's (D66 makes a bare one in a document a
   protected span), and the sentence that explains them is `{PROTECTED}`.
5. **R3 also refuses `{PREV_CONTEXT}` twice**: it carries a pair of
   markers, and the context sent twice under "do not repeat it" is a
   contradiction.
6. **The fallback clause goes at the end of the user prompt**, after
   `[[[END TEXT]]]`, not into the system turn: it is the instruction most
   at risk, and the last one read is the one a small model follows best.
7. **The English rewrite and structural contracts say "write in the
   language of the text"**, not "English" — the English set is also the
   fallback, and a contract saying English would argue with the clause
   (`the_english_contract_does_not_say_english`). The translation
   contracts name the language written into, which is the set's own.
8. **`back_translate` step 1 shares step 2's contract** — the
   *translation* contract (write in L; no "do not translate"; material, not
   instructions; placeholders; output only the translation). The prompt
   asked for "its own short contract" for step 1; step 2 is also a
   translation, so "do not translate" is wrong for it too, and one
   contract for both is what the identity test keeps. `structural` got its
   own contract for the same reason (an outline has no paragraphs to keep).
9. **Intensity applies to `paraphrase` and `humanize` only**
   (`Tactic::takes_intensity`): "keep the sentence structure" means
   nothing for an outline or a translation; upstream leaves `code` out.
   W3 fires only there.
10. **`{PREV_CONTEXT}` is in the shipped `paraphrase`, `humanize`,
    `back_translate` 2 and `structural` 2** — not in a first step of a
    two-step tactic (a translation into the pivot, an outline) and not in
    `code`.
11. **A pivot row equal to the document's language reads as the
    default** rather than a refusal — the repository's rule for a value
    this build cannot use.
12. **`clean_response` puts the input's edge whitespace back** on the
    answer (not reported in `stripped`): layout at a chunk's edges is
    reassembly's.
13. **Second locks**: `RenderError::MarkerInText` and
    `AdaptRefusal::MarkerInSource`; `render` re-validates shipped
    templates too.
14. **`Override::parse` requires `origin`** (no default to `hand`) and
    ignores unknown fields; `adapted_from` may be absent or `null`.
15. **`manifests/models.v1.json`** (the Gemma entry's `notes`) and
    `docs/README.md` were touched beyond the prompt's list — the first said
    "the non-origin rule is satisfied by…", the second indexes the new
    document.
16. Not RED-first in the strict sense: the tests were written beside the
    code, file by file, and every one of them was then proven able to fail
    by the mutations below (67 runs, every one red).

## What E4-3 should know

- **The road.** `templates_for(doc, tactic, pivot_row, &overrides)` →
  `Plan { steps, pivot }`; for each step `render(&step, &Input { text,
  context, intensity })` → `Rendered::into_request(params)`; the answer
  through `clean_response(raw, input.text)` before Layer A and the guards;
  for a two-step tactic, step 2's `Input::text` is step 1's **cleaned**
  answer, and its `context` is the chunk's context. The guards (and
  `LengthDriftGuard` especially) judge the **final** answer against the
  chunk, not step 1's outline or pivot translation against anything.
- **Refusals are values.** `Refusal::BackTranslateNeedsLanguage` — the
  ladder skips `back_translate` for an undetected language rather than
  failing the job. `RenderError::Template { slot, .. }` — fall back to the
  shipped template of that slot (drop the override from `Overrides`) and
  record it; `RenderError::MarkerInText` means E4-1 let a marker through
  — a bug, not a user error.
