**M1 — Compare's original pane selects with the mouse again** (`7a489e5`).
The pane was built `.disabled(true)`; in gpui-kit `next` a disabled
`Input` adds `capture_any_mouse_down(… stop_propagation)` over the field
(`crates/component/src/input/input.rs:778–780`), so a click neither
selected, focused nor reached the scrollbar. It is `.readonly(true)` now,
the library's mode for "focused, selected and copied, user changes
rejected". Read against the base state: typing and IME
(`replace_text_in_range`, `replace_and_mark_text_in_range`), paste,
delete, cut and undo/redo all return early on `!is_editable()`, which
readonly makes false; `insert` (`repaint_original`'s empty edit) and the
cursor placement (`set_cursor_position`, the following) run under
`with_edits_allowed` and still land — upstream's own
`test_readonly_rejects_user_edits_only` holds that, and
`the_original_is_asked_again_when_the_marks_move` still passes. No other
road was needed. Two tests in `compare.rs`:

- `the_original_selects_with_the_mouse` — a left-button drag across row 0
  of the original; asserts a non-empty selection and that the click
  focused it. **Red once** with `.disabled(true)` restored (the selection
  assertion), then put back.
- `typing_into_the_original_changes_nothing` — the same drag to focus it
  (asserted, so the keys cannot have gone elsewhere), then a typed `x`,
  Backspace, Delete, Enter, Tab, `Paste` over a filled clipboard, `Cut`
  and `Undo`; asserts the text unchanged. Red once with neither
  `readonly` nor `disabled` (the text changed); with `.disabled(true)` it
  fails earlier, on the focus.

The comments (`compare.rs` field docs and `repaint_original`) and
`docs/architecture/compare.md` said "disabled … still selects, copies and
searches"; they say read-only now, and the doc has a paragraph on why
not disabled. A read-only pane keeps the normal look rather than
disabled's half opacity — G6's glance. The verifier's probe,
`scripts/verify/gpui-bump/original-pane-mouse.sh`, is committed with it;
`VARIANT=disabled` now reproduces the finding on the fixed code
(`0..0` against the result's `39..95`), a plain run shows `39..95` on
both.

**L1 — our modals over the component's overlays** (`c5698ea`, D299).
gpui-kit `next` defers its popups at `POPUP_PRIORITY` 100 (a submenu
+1 over its parent; `crates/base/src/popup.rs:15`), its toasts at 101
(`crates/shell/src/root.rs:58`) and its tooltips at 200
(`crates/base/src/tooltip.rs:11`); GPUI paints and hit-tests deferred
draws in priority order across the window (`window.rs`
`paint_deferred_draws`). Our four modals were at 10. They are now:

- `dialog::MODAL_PRIORITY` = **1000** — the walk-through and the Report
  (`main.rs`) and Settings' Confirm. None holds a select, popover,
  tooltip or text field of the library's, so nothing of theirs has to
  show over them, and a help popover left open under "Run again" is
  covered, painted and hit alike.
- `dialog::FIELD_MODAL_PRIORITY` = **50** — Settings' Naming. Raising it
  too would break its name field: off macOS the field's right-click menu
  is `native_menu::fallback`, drawn by the component's `Root` at
  `POPUP_PRIORITY` (`crates/component/src/native_menu/fallback.rs:124`),
  in the window's first round of deferred draws like our dialog — so a
  dialog at 1000 would paint over the menu it just opened and take the
  click aimed at it. 50 is under the popups and over anything deferred at
  the default and upstream's own dialogs (`10 + layer`). Naming opens
  from the Save button, whose press has closed any select or popover on
  that page. This is the one departure from the task's "raise ours above
  the component's highest overlay", and why.

The test `dialog::tests::a_modal_is_over_every_overlay_the_library_defers`
asserts both at compile time against `gpui_base::POPUP_PRIORITY` (+10 for
submenus) and the tooltips' 200 — restated, because `TOOLTIP_PRIORITY` is
private (upstream's own test holds it above the popups). `gpui-base` is a
dev-dependency of the app for that one public number, which the
component does not re-export; it was already in the lock through the
component (one line of `Cargo.lock` moves: the app's dependency list).
**Red once** with `MODAL_PRIORITY` back at 10: the test target does not
compile, E0080 naming the assertion. What it cannot hold is the reason
