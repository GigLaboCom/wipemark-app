# Quarterly update from the apps team

The primary focus of this quarter remained on maintaining stability. For the initial time in 1 year, more bugs were resolved than created; additionally, following our shift of the database migration away from the main thread, the launch crash rate decreased by half.

During August, the updated onboarding process was released to all users. Even though the introductory tour remains unnecessarily lengthy and is slated for reduction to 2 screens, there has been a decrease in the number of individuals who quit prior to their initial sync.

Speed improvements were made to the search function within large folders. Because the index is currently constructed in the background and stays current during file modifications, the window no longer freezes for several seconds during the initial search following a restart.

Accessibility was also a priority during our development. Keyboard navigation is now possible for every setting control, while the dark theme colors meet the contrast requirements we established this past spring.

The redesign of the export feature was delayed until the following quarter, as we opted to release it later rather than twice because the file picker functioned inconsistently across various platforms. Things did not proceed exactly as intended.

For the upcoming quarter, our objectives include completing the export tasks, clarifying sync conflicts for better comprehension, and dedicating an entire week exclusively to addressing the minor frustrations reported by users.

We appreciate every person who participated in a testing session, provided responses to our survey, or reported a bug. Your contributions truly mattered.
